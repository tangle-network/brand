Add the Hospitality named theme: `data-theme="hospitality"` is a complete light scope, and with `.dark` on the same element it is a complete dark scope. Both are seeded from the canonical baselines in `tokens.css`, as `vault` is. It retints the surface ladder to sage, moves the accent family (primary, ring, accent text and wells, buttons, sidebar, brand accents) to forest green, and keeps every status and category tone canonical. The Hospitality app uses it in place of its local 166-property theme file.
