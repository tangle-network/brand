Add the Hospitality named theme: `[data-theme="hospitality"]` for light and `.dark[data-theme="hospitality"]` for dark. It retints the surface ladder to sage, moves the accent family (primary, ring, accent text and wells, buttons, sidebar, brand accents) to forest green, and keeps every status and category tone canonical. The Hospitality app uses it in place of its local 166-property theme file.
