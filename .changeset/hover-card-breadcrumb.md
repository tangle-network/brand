HoverCard and Breadcrumb: two high-value shadcn-convention components our products needed.

HoverCard: a rich hover preview (person, product, record) on the L4 overlay surface, lazy-mounted, Radix-backed. The face of every @mention, vendor name, and staff reference in a chat thread.

Breadcrumb: workspace navigation depth as one scrollable line — each crumb is a link except the last, which is the page. Fade-masked on narrow viewports, not wrapped.
